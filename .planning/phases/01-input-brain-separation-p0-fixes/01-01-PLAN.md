<objective>
Extract PlayerMovementBrain from PlayerInput.

Currently PlayerInput.cs owns FSM construction (InitializeStateMachine, lines 44–72), holds _stateMachine and _context, writes to context input booleans, and drives _stateMachine.Update() in its own Update. This mixes hardware reading, camera math, FSM ownership, and tick driving into one class.

After this plan, PlayerInput reads hardware and emits an InputFrame struct to Brain.ProcessFrame(). PlayerMovementBrain owns the StateMachine<MovementState>, PlayerMovementContext, InitializeStateMachine logic, and all context writes. PlayerInput.Update() calls Brain.ProcessFrame(frame) synchronously — the Brain has NO Update() of its own.

Purpose: Satisfies ARCH-01. Unblocks future movement system work by giving each class a single responsibility.
Output: PlayerMovementBrain.cs (new), PlayerInput.cs (stripped of FSM ownership).
</objective>
